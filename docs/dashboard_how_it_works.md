# How to read this dashboard (text for the dashboard's "How it works" box)

**What we're measuring.** Whether students who need extra support are being progress monitored on schedule in DIBELS.

**Who is counted.** Students currently enrolled in K-5 who scored Well Below or Below on the beginning-of-year benchmark. Each student is counted under their homeroom teacher.

**What counts as tested.** A student is tested in a window when they have at least 1 progress monitoring probe with a date inside that window.

**Schedules.**
- Well Below students: tested every 2 weeks.
- Below students: tested every 4 weeks.

**Target.** 95% of students tested by the end of each window.

**Checkpoints.** Each window has checkpoints along the way. The checkpoint marked CURRENT is the target a grade or teacher is compared to today.
- On track: at or above the current checkpoint.
- Behind: up to 15 points below it.
- Off track: more than 15 points below it.
- When a window closes: Met (95% or more) or Missed.

**Drill-down.** Click a grade to see its teachers, then a teacher to see which students still need a probe.

**Data as of.** The DIBELS export can lag by a few days. A probe given recently may not show yet. The "data as of" date at the top tells you how current the numbers are.
